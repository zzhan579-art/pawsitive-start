Building a healthy daily routine

A consistent routine helps a dog understand what to expect. Feeding, bathroom breaks, exercise, rest, and grooming should fit your dog's age, health, and energy level.

Feeding and water

Serve appropriate dog food in measured portions and keep fresh water available. Ask your veterinarian about the amount and schedule that match your dog.

Exercise and play

Walks and safe play provide physical activity and mental stimulation. Increase activity gradually and watch for signs of fatigue.

Grooming

Brush your dog's coat as needed, check the paws and ears, and introduce tooth brushing slowly with dog-safe products.

Rest and bathroom breaks

Provide a quiet place to sleep. Offer bathroom breaks at consistent times, especially after waking, eating, playing, and before bedtime.

A simple routine to start

Begin the morning with a bathroom break, breakfast, and a short walk. Include rest and play during the day, then finish with dinner and a final bathroom break before bed.
