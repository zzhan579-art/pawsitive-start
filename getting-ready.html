Getting ready for your new dog

Preparing before your dog arrives can make the first few days calmer and safer. Start with essential supplies, choose a quiet resting area, and remove objects that could be chewed or swallowed.

Prepare the living space

Choose an easy-to-clean area for food and water. Set up a comfortable bed away from busy walkways, and use gates when your dog needs a smaller supervised space.

Plan the first day

Keep the introduction quiet and allow time to explore. Show your dog the water bowl, sleeping area, and bathroom location. Begin with a predictable schedule instead of too many visitors.

New dog supply checklist

This table organizes the basic items a new owner should prepare before bringing a dog home.

Item Purpose Preparation Notes
Food and water bowls Provide a consistent place for meals and water. Choose easy-to-clean bowls and a stable location.
Leash and harness Support safe, supervised outdoor walks. Check the size and fit before the first walk.
Dog bed or crate Create a comfortable place to rest. Set it up in a quiet area before the dog arrives.

Remember

Ask the shelter, rescue, or previous owner about the dog's current food and routine. Gradual changes can help reduce stress during the transition.
