Basic Training for Your Dog

Training helps you communicate with your dog and build good habits together. Start with short, positive sessions and reward the behaviors you want to see again.

Use Positive Reinforcement

Reward your dog with praise or a small treat right after a behavior you want to encourage. Keep training calm and enjoyable.

Teach “Sit”

Hold a treat near your dog's nose and move it slowly upward. When your dog sits, say “sit” and give the reward.

Practice “Come”

Begin in a quiet, safe area. Say your dog's name and “come” in a friendly voice, then reward your dog for reaching you.

Keep Sessions Short

Practice for a few minutes at a time and repeat regularly. End on a positive note so your dog stays interested in learning.

Training tip: Be patient

Dogs learn at different speeds. Practice one skill at a time, reward small improvements, and take a break if your dog becomes distracted. Consistency and patience help build trust.
