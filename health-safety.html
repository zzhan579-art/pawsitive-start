Keeping Your Dog Healthy and Safe

Regular veterinary care and a safer home environment help protect your dog. This guide introduces simple habits for new owners, including preparing for vet visits, checking household hazards, and knowing when to ask for professional help.

Plan Veterinary Visits

Schedule regular checkups. Bring any vaccination or medical records you have, and ask your veterinarian about preventive care that fits your dog.

Check Your Home

Keep medications, cleaning products, and unsafe foods where your dog cannot reach them. Check new plants and household items before leaving them near your dog.

Stay Safe Outdoors

Use a secure leash where needed, carry water on outings, and watch your dog for signs that it needs a rest. Keep identification information up to date.

Prepare for Emergencies

Save the contact information for your veterinarian and a nearby emergency clinic. If you think your dog swallowed something harmful, contact a veterinary professional promptly.

Keep Important Information Handy

Keep your dog's medical records and your veterinarian's phone number in an easy-to-find place. If you notice a concerning change in your dog's health or behavior, contact your veterinarian for advice.
